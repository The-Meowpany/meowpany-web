Hiring custom software has a basic problem: until you see it working, you can't tell whether the provider understood what you need. So we changed the order. **First we show you your idea working; only then do we talk about price.**

## How it works

1. **A 30-minute call.** You tell us the problem, who will use the solution and what the first version must do. Right after, we send you that scope in writing.
2. **Your first version within 72 hours** of agreeing on that scope. It actually works, it isn't a mockup: you open it with a private link and try it with your team.
3. **You decide.** If it convinces you, we send a fixed price and carry on. If not, that's the end of it: you pay nothing and we won't chase you.

## What it includes, and what not

The main agreed features, working with sample data. It doesn't yet include loading your real data, publishing to app stores or paid third-party services. We build one first version per client and project.

## If you go ahead

You get the specification, a fixed price and a timeline. You pay 50 % to continue and 50 % on delivery, and what's already built becomes the base of the project.
