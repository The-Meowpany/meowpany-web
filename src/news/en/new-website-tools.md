Our new website explains what we do, how we work and what it costs, and includes four tools that give you a concrete result without signing up.

- **[Website audit](/en/audit/):** performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO measured on a phone, with fixes ranked by impact, using Google PageSpeed Insights.
- **[Project estimator](/en/estimate/):** describe your system in plain words and get a printable proposal with scope, hours, timeline and price range.
- **[Return calculator](/en/roi/):** what manual work, errors and lost sales cost you every month, and how many months the project takes to pay for itself. Calculated in your browser.
- **[Call booking](/en/book/):** pick a day and time for a 30-minute video call, shown in your time zone.
